# Service Level Agreement — ZIMAM (template)

*Template pending commercial/legal review. Adjust tiers and credits to your support model.*

## Support channels

- **Starter**: email support, 2 business-day response, community docs
- **Pro**: email + priority queue, 1 business-day response
- **Enterprise**: named contact, 4-hour response during business hours, quarterly review

## Covered

- Defects in the shipped binaries reproducible on supported Windows Server versions
- License key issuance/renewal issues
- Upgrade assistance between published versions

## Not covered

- Custom integration development (guacd gateway, AD/LDAP, recordings — roadmap items)
- Issues caused by modification of binaries, unsupported OS versions, or third-party software
- Data recovery beyond the built-in backup/restore tooling

## Availability

The software runs on your infrastructure; ZIMAM does not host the console for you, so console availability is your responsibility. Vendor-side commitments apply to license services and update distribution only.

## Remedies

For verified defects unresolved within 30 days of report, ZIMAM may provide a fix in the next release or, at its discretion for Enterprise customers, a service credit.
